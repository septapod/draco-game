Chapter I

The World of Draco

A world where humans — called Dragonette Keepers — bond with, train, and race baby dragons called dragonettes.

The name "Draco" comes from the Latin word for dragon. Despite their name, dragonettes are not small — they hatch from eggs at full adult dragon size. They are "baby" dragons in age and experience, but massive in stature.

Bond

The connection between Keeper and dragonette is everything. At its deepest, a Keeper can transform into their dragon.

Race

Enter stadiums, compete against rivals, use elemental powers as strategy. Win badges to prove your worth.

Master

Earn 10 racing badges, then challenge Draco — the powerful Thunder Cloud Dragon and raid boss.

World Rules

Dragon Biology

Hatch at full adult size. Crystal slots on wings. Breathe colored fire matching their element.

Society

Fire dragons are most common. An organized racing circuit with stadiums and rules.

Technology

Holographic communication, advanced robotics. Magic and technology blend seamlessly.

Day/Night Cycle

Most abilities work anytime. "Silver Moonlight" only at night. More secrets unlock with Thunder Cloud.

1000 Elements

There are "like 1000 elements" in the Draco universe — meaning roughly 1000 different types of dragons. Most remain locked and undiscovered. "Locked elements that I'm not supposed to tell you about, their secret." The element system is open-ended, with new elements continuously discoverable.

Dragons of different elements can breed, producing hybrid dragonettes with combined powers. To tame a breed dragon, you need a Breed Berry — a special berry specifically for bonding with hybrid dragonettes.

Known breed: Spirit-Grass — combines Spirit truth-sensing with Grass powers. Strong against Thunder Cloud. Has "Grass Spirit Army" attack (drains opponents by 10%).

Custom Element Rule

Every player MUST invent their own unique element for at least one dragon. Creativity and personal expression are built into the game.

Example: Aza created "Power Breathing" — an ability that degrades the attack power of every opposing dragon by 10 points.

Chapter V

The Racing Circuit

Racing is the primary competitive activity in Draco and the main way to earn badges toward becoming a Dragonette Master.

Structure

Races take place in stadiums built by Aloha. She also announces and hosts them.

Modes

Solo Mode or Team Mode. Teammates share abilities — if one creates an obstacle, teammates pass through it.

Winning

A trophy appears and begins flashing. Fly through it while visible. Timing is critical.

Powers Allowed

Use elemental powers during races: leaf walls, illusions, obstacles. Strategy is part of the game.

BANNED FROM RACES

Speed

270 mph — 5x faster than normal dragons

Power

All abilities + matching Speed dragon top speed

Poisonous

Poisonous powers (from snake snacks) — not allowed

Race Drone

A drone announces races and manages countdowns. Can change color for special occasions — like turning pink for a special event.

Trophy Variations

Different races award different trophies. Each is named and styled per event — like the "Supersonic Pink Trophy."

Cheating & Enforcement

Wonky Donkers always tries to disguise banned dragons with cardboard. Spirit dragons detect disguises (truth-sensing). Psychic dragons remove them (making cardboard vanish). Aloha enforces rules by literally kicking cheaters out with a giant robotic boot.

Chapter VI

Combat & Battle

Attack Power System

Every dragon starts at 100% attack power. Abilities like "Power Breathing" degrade opponent power by 10 per hit. At 0%, the dragon is eliminated. After battle, all dragons recharge to 100%.

Shelled Enemies

Enemies with dragon scale energy shells are extremely durable. Non-fire attacks deal ~1% damage (100 hits needed). Fire attacks: only 2 hits to break through. Use Fire dragons.

Poisonous Powers

Obtained via snake snacks from the snake friend encounter. Grants toxic attacks for use in combat and adventure. Banned from races but effective in battle.

Clone Mechanic

Some enemies (like Jack O'Rabbit) create clones when attacked. Clones grow from baby to adult in ~2 seconds, powered by Earth's magnetism. Counter: throw them into space (psychic powers or Speed Atmosphere) where they can't grow. Fire causes more clones. Universe element one-hits shelled enemies.

Chapter VII

Heroes & Villains

Aloha

Robot / Host

Female humanoid robot — "Squeakers' Sister." Gray, with upside-down laundry basket head, glowing orange eyes, very long neck, expandable mouth, two arms that cycle over the head. Not as big as a human. Beeps a lot.

"Aloha is the racing world."

Draco

Final Boss / Raid Boss

Thunder Cloud Dragon. Not inherently evil — controlled by an evil shell connected to a mysterious voice. Once defeated, freed and can be tamed. Like a "mega Rayquaza in a Pokémon boss stadium."

Weakness: Grass. Requires 10 badges to challenge.

Wonky Donkers

Recurring Villain

A skinny guy with huge cheeks, tiny eyebrows, black hair, and tan skin. Always tries to disguise banned dragons using cardboard. It never works. Bumbling, not very bright, once accidentally announced to everyone that he had a Power dragon.

The Tow Road

World Boss

Giant evil flying turtle with dragon-scale-energy shell. Has its own dimension with special berries. Drops dragon scale and turtle meat when defeated.

Weakness: Fire (2 hits). Non-fire: ~1% damage per hit.

The Evil Groundhog

Adventure Enemy

A very, very long evil groundhog with a shell and wings. Shell made of dragon scale energy. Drops dragon scale when defeated. Fire attacks recommended.

Jack O'Rabbit

Adventure Enemy

A giant fuzzy rabbit with a skull for a head. The skull acts as a shield. No shell — but creates clones when attacked. Clones grow to adult size in ~2 seconds via Earth's magnetism. Counter: throw them into space.

Drops: Rabbit meat, dragon skill, rabbit skull (summons a skeleton Dragon).

Snake Friend

Ally

A friendly snake found during adventure. Offers glowing snake snacks that grant poisonous powers. A valuable encounter — but remember, poisonous powers are banned from races.

The Finisher

Team Member

A person (not a dragon) who joins the player's racing team. A named ally who races alongside you in team events.

Keeper Transformation

The deepest level of bonding. With a Dragon Eye Amulet and sufficient bond, a Keeper can physically transform into one of their dragons — gaining all powers while retaining their own mind.

If you transform into a Thunder Cloud dragon, you gain lightning feathers that generate real thunder and lightning.

Story Moments

A villain disguised a Speed dragon using cardboard to sneak it into a race. A Spirit dragon sensed the deception and alerted Aloha, who reported the dragon was going 100 mph faster than it should. A Psychic dragon then made all cardboard on the track disappear, revealing the costume.

Themes: Teamwork, truth vs. deception, the unique value of each dragon type.

One specific wild Spirit dragon who had lost its family approached Brent — the first kind person it saw — wanting to be adopted. This particular dragon craved companionship, and its wing crystal slots filled automatically because Brent showed it kindness.

Themes: Found family, compassion, the emotional bond between keeper and dragon.

A flying turtle with dragon-scale-energy shell attacked. A Psychic dragon tried throwing a tree — barely 1% damage. The keepers realized the shell is weak to fire. Two fire-breath hits defeated it, harvesting dragon scale and turtle meat.

Themes: Problem-solving, learning enemy weaknesses, teamwork between dragon types.

After obtaining dragon scale from the Tow Road, keepers placed it on their dragons to activate force fields. They tested by firing attacks at each other — the fields absorbed and reflected them.

Themes: Preparation, trust between teammates, testing before the real fight.

Two keepers entered as a team — Grass dragon (Leaf Blade) and Spirit dragon. The Grass dragon created a razor-leaf wall, but teammates could pass through. Wonky Donkers showed up with a cardboard-disguised Power dragon and was detected. The team won by flying through the flashing trophy, earning five badges.

Themes: Friendship, strategy, humor, the thrill of competition.

A keeper with a deep bond received a Dragon Eye Amulet — a glowstone containing the eye of a dragon — granting the ability to transform into any bonded dragonette while keeping their own mind.

Themes: Earned trust, transformation, the reward of deep bonds.

The keepers encountered Jack O'Rabbit — a giant fuzzy rabbit with a skull for a head. Brent used psychic powers to float a carrot as a distraction, and the creature took a big bite. When attacked, it began creating clones that grew to adult size in seconds.

Brent threw clones into space with psychic powers, discovering they need Earth's magnetism to grow. Ramming clones barely scratched them (1% damage). Fire worked but caused more clones. Finally, Speed Atmosphere launched Jack O'Rabbit into space — victory. Loot: rabbit meat, a dragon skill, and a skull that summons a skeleton Dragon you can tame.

Themes: Creative problem-solving, environmental mechanics, learning weaknesses through trial and error.

Before a race, the keepers discovered a rare Spirit-Grass breed dragon nearby. Aza recognized its value: "Thunder Cloud is weak to it, but also it can tell true." They tamed it with a Breed Berry — a special berry for bonding with hybrid dragonettes.

Themes: Recognizing strategic advantages, breed diversity, preparation before battle.

The team — Aza with Flash Dragon, Brent with Spirit and Psychic dragons, and The Finisher — entered a special race. The Race Drone turned pink for the occasion. They used the Spirit-Grass breed's "Grass Spirit Army" attack (10% drain), won the Supersonic Pink Trophy, and earned five badges toward Dragonette Master.

Themes: Teamwork, special occasions, hybrid dragon strategy, progression toward mastery.

Chapter IX

Keeper Secrets

Game balance, restrictions, and mysteries yet to be revealed...

Why Not Always Choose Power?

  • Weaker individual abilities than specialists
  • Vulnerable to Thunder Cloud (the final boss)
  • Banned from races (can't earn badges)
  • Specialists excel in their domain

Spirit Dragons in Races

Spirit dragons are NOT banned. While they sense truth and detect deception, this doesn't give unfair racing advantage — tricks and cheating are already against the rules. Obstacles are allowed as normal strategy.

Power Dragon Flexibility

Power dragon keepers can cycle through element types. Using a spell, "put the berry back" — revert to egg state — and re-bond as a different element.

Force Field Sensitivity

Dragon Scale force fields have a sensitivity percentage that decreases with use. Starts high, degrades as it absorbs hits. Can reflect absorbed attacks back at the attacker.

Breed Dragons

Dragons of different elements can breed, creating hybrid dragonettes with combined powers. Tame them with a Breed Berry. Known: Spirit-Grass Breed — has "Grass Spirit Army" attack and is strong against Thunder Cloud.

1000 Elements

There are "like 1000 elements" — most are locked and undiscovered. "Locked elements that I'm not supposed to tell you about, their secret." Universe, Cosmic, Egg are just the beginning. Wood and Starlight remain mysterious.
