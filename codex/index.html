Chapter I

The World of Draco

A world where humans — called Dragonette Keepers — bond with, train, and race baby dragons called dragonettes.

The name "Draco" comes from the Latin word for dragon. Despite their name, dragonettes are not small — they hatch from eggs at full adult dragon size. They are "baby" dragons in age and experience, but massive in stature.

Bond

The connection between Keeper and dragonette is everything. At its deepest, a Keeper can transform into their dragon.

Race

Enter stadiums, compete against rivals, use elemental powers as strategy. Win badges to prove your worth.

Master

Earn 10 racing badges, then challenge Draco — the powerful Thunder Cloud Dragon and raid boss.

World Rules

Dragon Biology

Hatch at full adult size. Crystal slots on wings. Breathe colored fire matching their element.

Society

Fire dragons are most common. An organized racing circuit with stadiums and rules.

Technology

Holographic communication, advanced robotics. Magic and technology blend seamlessly.

Day/Night Cycle

Most abilities work anytime. "Silver Moonlight" only at night. More secrets unlock with Thunder Cloud.

Beyond the Horizon

Aurora Layer

A shimmering band of light in the upper atmosphere. Gateway to the Chromatic Fields. Visible from the ground, but only reachable by flying dragons.

Chromatic Fields

A dimensional intersection with floating islands and prismatic skies. Dragons born here have "wrong" colors — their element stays the same but appearance shifts.

Shadowpeak Mountains

Dark range under perpetual storms. The Voice Below's obsidian fortresses pulse with purple energy. Shadow dragons patrol the skies.

1000 Elements

There are "like 1000 elements" in the Draco universe — meaning roughly 1000 different types of dragons. Most remain locked and undiscovered. "Locked elements that I'm not supposed to tell you about, their secret." The element system is open-ended, with new elements continuously discoverable. Starlight was recently unlocked!

Dragons of different elements can breed, producing hybrid dragonettes with combined powers. To tame a breed dragon, you need a Breed Berry — a special berry specifically for bonding with hybrid dragonettes.

Known breed: Spirit-Grass — combines Spirit truth-sensing with Grass powers. Strong against Thunder Cloud. Has "Grass Spirit Army" attack (drains opponents by 10%).

Chromatic Dragons

Dragons born in the Chromatic Fields — a dimensional intersection accessible through the Aurora Layer — keep their elemental powers but have "wrong" colors. A Fire dragon might be green, an Electric dragon blue, a Water dragon pink.

Their element remains unchanged; only their appearance differs. Rare and highly prized because their misleading colors confuse opponents in battle.

Legendary Elements

One element in the Draco universe is reserved for a legendary dragon: Mountain. It belongs to Nalia, the first documented Draco Legend. Mountain dragons are mountain-sized and mountain-shaped. The world treats them the way real mountains are treated, only more so, because the world knows they are dragons.

Powers and matchups are TBD by Aza. See the Draco Legends chapter for Nalia's full canon.

Custom Element Rule

Every player MUST invent their own unique element for at least one dragon. Creativity and personal expression are built into the game.

Example: Aza created "Power Breathing" — an ability that degrades the attack power of every opposing dragon by 10 points.

Chapter V

The Racing Circuit

Racing is the primary competitive activity in Draco and the main way to earn badges toward becoming a Dragonette Master.

Structure

Races take place in stadiums built by Aloha. She also announces and hosts them.

Modes

Solo Mode or Team Mode. Teammates share abilities — if one creates an obstacle, teammates pass through it.

Winning

A trophy appears and begins flashing. Fly through it while visible. Timing is critical.

Full-Contact Racing

ALL dragon abilities are allowed and encouraged — combat attacks, health-draining moves, elemental powers, obstacles. Use your full arsenal.

Cheating & Enforcement

Wonky Donkers always tries to disguise banned dragons with cardboard. Spirit dragons detect disguises (truth-sensing). Psychic dragons remove them (making cardboard vanish). Aloha enforces rules by literally kicking cheaters out with a giant robotic boot.

Chapter VI

Combat & Battle

Attack Power System

Every dragon starts at 100% attack power. Abilities like "Power Breathing" degrade opponent power by 10 per hit. At 0%, the dragon is eliminated. After battle, all dragons recharge to 100%.

Shelled Enemies

Enemies with dragon scale energy shells are extremely durable. Non-fire attacks deal ~1% damage (100 hits needed). Fire attacks: only 2 hits to break through. Use Fire dragons.

Poisonous Powers

Obtained via snake snacks from the snake friend encounter. Grants toxic attacks for use in combat and adventure. Banned from races but effective in battle.

Clone Mechanic

Some enemies (like Jack O'Rabbit) create clones when attacked. Clones grow from baby to adult in ~2 seconds, powered by Earth's magnetism. Counter: throw them into space (psychic powers or Speed Atmosphere) where they can't grow. Fire causes more clones. Universe element one-hits shelled enemies.

Chapter VII

Heroes & Villains

Aloha

Robot / Host

Female humanoid robot — "Squeakers' Sister." Gray, with upside-down laundry basket head, glowing orange eyes, very long neck, expandable mouth, two arms that cycle over the head. Not as big as a human. Beeps a lot.

"Aloha is the racing world."

Draco

Final Boss / Raid Boss

Thunder Cloud Dragon. Not inherently evil — controlled by an evil shell connected to the Voice Below. Once defeated, freed and can be tamed. Like a "mega Rayquaza in a Pokémon boss stadium."

Weakness: Grass. Requires 10 badges to challenge.

Wonky Donkers

Recurring Villain

A skinny guy with huge cheeks, tiny eyebrows, black hair, and tan skin. Always tries to disguise banned dragons using cardboard. It never works. Bumbling, not very bright, once accidentally announced to everyone that he had a Power dragon.

The Tow Road

World Boss

Giant evil flying turtle with dragon-scale-energy shell. Has its own dimension with special berries. Drops dragon scale and turtle meat when defeated.

Weakness: Fire (2 hits). Non-fire: ~1% damage per hit.

The Evil Groundhog

Adventure Enemy

A very, very long evil groundhog with a shell and wings. Shell made of dragon scale energy. Drops dragon scale when defeated. Fire attacks recommended.

Jack O'Rabbit

Adventure Enemy

A giant fuzzy rabbit with a skull for a head. The skull acts as a shield. No shell — but creates clones when attacked. Clones grow to adult size in ~2 seconds via Earth's magnetism. Counter: throw them into space.

Drops: Rabbit meat, dragon skill, rabbit skull (summons a skeleton Dragon).

Snake Friend

Ally

A friendly snake found during adventure. Offers glowing snake snacks that grant poisonous powers — effective in combat and adventure, but poisonous powers are the one ability type banned from races.

Rainbow Tortoise

Adventure Enemy

Massive turtle-like creature with a dragon-scale-energy shell that shimmers in rainbow colors. Guards Power crystals in Rainbow Valley caves. Vulnerable to fire (2 hits) or 100 regular hits.

Forest Bear

Ally / Creature

House-sized bear with moss-covered brown fur and glowing green eyes. Massive claws, incredibly strong. Despite its fearsome appearance, can be gentle if treated with kindness.

Forest Stag

Ally / Creature

Majestic stag with antlers made of living branches that sprout fresh leaves. Amber eyes, can communicate telepathically with forest helpers. Leader of forest creatures, ancient and wise.

Marcus (The Finisher)

Team Member

Formerly a middle-tier racer who found his true purpose helping an injured dragon. Renamed himself "The Finisher" after discovering his talent for strong race conclusions. Values fairness, perseverance, and heart over raw talent. A loyal teammate who respects strategic racing.

Star Guardians

Allies

Fire and Electric dragons who guard star systems from shadow threats. They patrol the upper atmosphere and beyond, serving as the first line of defense against Star Parasites and the Voice Below's forces.

Star Parasites

Enemy Creature

Gigantic shadow creatures that wrap around stars and drain their light. Dozens of glowing purple eyes and mouths. Commanded by the Voice Below. Defeating one restores the star it was draining.

Surveillance Drones

Enemy

Mechanical drones disguised as dragons. A single glowing purple eye gives away their true nature. Used by the Voice Below for spying — not powerful in combat, but they report keeper movements back to the Shadowpeak Mountains.

The Voice Below

Ultimate Antagonist

The greater villain behind Draco's corruption. Commands an army of Shadow dragons, Star Parasites, and Surveillance Drones from obsidian fortresses in the Shadowpeak Mountains. Its goal: consume starlight from stars, draining the universe of light itself.

True origin and nature remain unknown. Controls Draco through the evil shell — but Draco is merely one piece of a larger campaign.

Keeper Transformation

The deepest level of bonding. With a Dragon Eye Amulet and sufficient bond, a Keeper can physically transform into one of their dragons — gaining all powers while retaining their own mind.

If you transform into a Thunder Cloud dragon, you gain lightning feathers that generate real thunder and lightning.

Story Moments

A villain disguised a Speed dragon using cardboard to sneak it into a race. A Spirit dragon sensed the deception and alerted Aloha, who reported the dragon was going 100 mph faster than it should. A Psychic dragon then made all cardboard on the track disappear, revealing the costume.

Themes: Teamwork, truth vs. deception, the unique value of each dragon type.

One specific wild Spirit dragon who had lost its family approached Papa — the first kind person it saw — wanting to be adopted. This particular dragon craved companionship, and its wing crystal slots filled automatically because Papa showed it kindness.

Themes: Found family, compassion, the emotional bond between keeper and dragon.

A flying turtle with dragon-scale-energy shell attacked. A Psychic dragon tried throwing a tree — barely 1% damage. The keepers realized the shell is weak to fire. Two fire-breath hits defeated it, harvesting dragon scale and turtle meat.

Themes: Problem-solving, learning enemy weaknesses, teamwork between dragon types.

After obtaining dragon scale from the Tow Road, keepers placed it on their dragons to activate force fields. They tested by firing attacks at each other — the fields absorbed and reflected them.

Themes: Preparation, trust between teammates, testing before the real fight.

Two keepers entered as a team — Grass dragon (Leaf Blade) and Spirit dragon. The Grass dragon created a razor-leaf wall, but teammates could pass through. Wonky Donkers showed up with a cardboard-disguised Power dragon and was detected. The team won by flying through the flashing trophy, earning five badges.

Themes: Friendship, strategy, humor, the thrill of competition.

A keeper with a deep bond received a Dragon Eye Amulet — a glowstone containing the eye of a dragon — granting the ability to transform into any bonded dragonette while keeping their own mind.

Themes: Earned trust, transformation, the reward of deep bonds.

The keepers encountered Jack O'Rabbit — a giant fuzzy rabbit with a skull for a head. Papa used psychic powers to float a carrot as a distraction, and the creature took a big bite. When attacked, it began creating clones that grew to adult size in seconds.

Papa threw clones into space with psychic powers, discovering they need Earth's magnetism to grow. Ramming clones barely scratched them (1% damage). Fire worked but caused more clones. Finally, Speed Atmosphere launched Jack O'Rabbit into space — victory. Loot: rabbit meat, a dragon skill, and a skull that summons a skeleton Dragon you can tame.

Themes: Creative problem-solving, environmental mechanics, learning weaknesses through trial and error.

Before a race, the keepers discovered a rare Spirit-Grass breed dragon nearby. Aza recognized its value: "Thunder Cloud is weak to it, but also it can tell true." They tamed it with a Breed Berry — a special berry for bonding with hybrid dragonettes.

Themes: Recognizing strategic advantages, breed diversity, preparation before battle.

The team — Aza with Flash Dragon, Papa with Spirit and Psychic dragons, and The Finisher — entered a special race. The Race Drone turned pink for the occasion. They used the Spirit-Grass breed's "Grass Spirit Army" attack (10% drain), won the Supersonic Pink Trophy, and earned five badges toward Dragonette Master.

Themes: Teamwork, special occasions, hybrid dragon strategy, progression toward mastery.

Chapter IX

Draco Legends

Draco Legends are dragons whose story has grown beyond their own life into the shape of the world.

They are not stronger than other dragons just because they are legendary. They are legendary because what they are and what they have done has become inseparable from the world itself. There are very few of them. The world remembers each one.

Draco himself is not a legend in this sense. He is the namesake of the game and the ultimate raid boss, but he is not in this chapter. Power Up is not a legend either. New legends will be added over time, made up by Aza.

At launch the Legend Codex holds one.

LEGEND

Nalia

Pronounced NAH-lee-yah. She/her.

Mountain

Baby Form

About the size of a loaf of bread. A blue cylinder body with two wings, two tiny claws, two tiny paws, and two very cute eyes. She wears a chest full of medals from the contests she kept winning.

Canonical visual pending. Aza is drawing baby Nalia on paper for the Codex.

Marcus, the ally now known as The Finisher, found Nalia when he was a kid. He cared for her when she was tiny. She kept winning awards: roughly a thousand for Dragon Racing, roughly a thousand for Cutest Dragon, until she could barely move under the weight of the medals.

One night Marcus went to bed, and in the morning Nalia was the size of a mountain. The bond was deeper too. No external trigger, no dream, no visitor, no noise. Just natural growth at the right moment. When Nalia was big enough, Marcus released her into the world.

Now she sleeps. For two million years she is a literal mountain. A forest grows on her back. The snow on top is just snow until she wakes, at which point it lifts off the peak and becomes her wings. Caves run through her. People know she is a legendary dragon and treat her better than a regular mountain: they cultivate forests on her slopes rather than build buildings. Exactly one structure stands on her, an animal shelter. That is the only exception.

Every two million years she wakes for one year. During her wake she helps other dragons, flies, visits Marcus, and fights bad guys. She fights The Voice Below, the main, main villain, and Wonky Donkers, the goofball. Then she sleeps again.

  • Tamer: Marcus (The Finisher).
  • Awards: nearly a thousand in Dragon Racing, nearly a thousand in Cutest Dragon.
  • Sleep cycle: two million years asleep, one year awake, repeats.
  • Reproduction: none. There is only one Nalia, and she practically lives forever.
  • Diet: fruit from the Chromatic Fields, scaled for a dragon her size. Normal fruit is too small. She would need more than a million pieces of ordinary fruit to be satisfied.
  • Bond: can be a team's best friend or one player's best friend. Player choice.

How to Find Her

Baby Nalia cannot be met. That era is closed.

Mountain Nalia can be found and tamed, with one rule. While seeking her, a Keeper cannot bring a legendary dragon or a Chromatic dragon in their active party. Owning these dragons is fine. They just cannot travel with you on the Nalia journey.

Chromatic Variant

A multicolored Nalia exists in the Chromatic Fields. Finding her requires luck. Her typing is TBD by Aza (still Mountain, or something more).

More legends will be added here over time. Aza decides which dragons earn the title.

Chapter X

Keeper Secrets

Game balance, restrictions, and mysteries yet to be revealed...

Why Not Always Choose Power?

  • Weaker individual abilities than specialists
  • Vulnerable to Thunder Cloud (the final boss)
  • Banned from races (can't earn badges)
  • Specialists excel in their domain

Spirit Dragons in Races

Spirit dragons are NOT banned. While they sense truth and detect deception, this doesn't give unfair racing advantage — tricks and cheating are already against the rules. Races are full-contact — all dragon abilities including combat and health-draining moves are part of normal racing strategy.

Power Dragon Flexibility

Power dragon keepers can cycle through element types. Using a spell, "put the berry back" — revert to egg state — and re-bond as a different element.

Force Field Sensitivity

Dragon Scale force fields have a sensitivity percentage that decreases with use. Starts high, degrades as it absorbs hits. Can reflect absorbed attacks back at the attacker.

Breed Dragons

Dragons of different elements can breed, creating hybrid dragonettes with combined powers. Tame them with a Breed Berry. Known: Spirit-Grass Breed — has "Grass Spirit Army" attack and is strong against Thunder Cloud.

1000 Elements

There are "like 1000 elements" — most are locked and undiscovered. "Locked elements that I'm not supposed to tell you about, their secret." Universe, Cosmic, Egg are just the beginning. Starlight has been unlocked — Wood remains mysterious.

The Shadow Element

An enemy-only element serving the Voice Below. Shadow dragons have dark bodies with glowing purple veins and command dark energy. They can drain energy with Shadow Drain and merge — three combining into one giant with six purple eyes. Weak to Cosmic, Starlight, and Spirit. No keeper has ever bonded one... yet.

The Voice Below

The entity behind Draco's corruption is far more dangerous than anyone knew. Called "the Voice Below," it commands Shadow dragons, Star Parasites, and Surveillance Drones from obsidian fortresses in the Shadowpeak Mountains. Its goal: consume starlight from the stars themselves.

Shadowpeak Mountains — Dark mountain range under perpetual storms. Location of the Voice Below's obsidian fortresses veined with purple energy.
Silver Moonlight — The only known power that only works at nighttime. Others unlock with Thunder Cloud.
Snake Friend — Ally encounter. Friendly snake that gives players snake snacks, granting poisonous powers.
Snake Snacks — Items from the snake friend. Grant poisonous powers. Banned from races.
Star Guardian — Fire and Electric dragons who guard star systems from shadow threats. Allies patrolling the upper atmosphere against the Voice Below.
Star Parasite — Gigantic shadow creature that wraps around stars and drains their light. Dozens of purple eyes and mouths. Commanded by the Voice Below.
Speed Berry — Golden shimmering berry that grants a temporary speed boost for a single race.
Special Berries — Rare berries from the Tow Road's dimension. Make dragons slightly faster.
Speed Atmosphere — Combat move that launches enemies into space. Counter to magnetism-dependent enemies.
Grass Spirit Army — Attack unique to Spirit-Grass breed. Drains opponents by 10% per use.
Spirit-Grass Breed — Hybrid dragon combining Spirit truth-sensing with Grass powers. Strong against Thunder Cloud.
Starlight Dragon — Deep blue to violet scales with silver star patterns. Creates concentrated starlight beams, illuminates darkness, temporarily blinds opponents. Crystalline spines create wind-chime sounds. Has the Diamonds in the Sky attack.
Surveillance Drone — Mechanical drone disguised as a dragon. Single glowing purple eye. Used by the Voice Below for spying. Not powerful in combat but dangerous for intelligence gathering.
Supersonic Pink Trophy — A named race trophy. Different races award different types.
The Crystal — Artifact that reveals a dragon's powers when looked through at the dragon.
The Finisher — See Marcus (The Finisher).
The Voice Below — The greater villain behind Draco's corruption. Commands Shadow dragons, Star Parasites, and Surveillance Drones from obsidian fortresses in the Shadowpeak Mountains. Goal: consume starlight from stars.
Tow Road — Giant evil flying turtle. Shell made of dragon scale energy. Has its own dimension.
Thunder Cloud Dragon — A dragon of the Thunder Cloud element. Draco is the most powerful, but others exist. Not all have shells.
Universal Camouflage — Universe dragon ability. Bends light for full invisibility.
Universe Dragon — Bears a microscopic universe map with a tiny compass. Can crush shelled creatures in one hit. Has the Universal Camouflage ability.
Wooden Whistle — Carved whistle from a grateful Forest Bear. May summon forest creatures or call for help in the woods.
Wonky Donkers — Bumbling recurring villain. Skinny, huge cheeks, tiny eyebrows, black hair, tan skin. Disguises banned dragons with cardboard.
