## New Elements

### Universe
- Dragon body bears a microscopic map of the entire universe — need a microscope to see individual stars
- Has a tiny, microscopic, skinny compass on the map
- **Power:** Can crush shelled creatures with a single hit (unique among elements)
- Distinct from Cosmic: Universe = compass appearance; Cosmic = space map

### Cosmic
- Looks like "a map of space" on the dragon
- Powers basically the same as Universe
- Visually distinct: Cosmic = space map; Universe = compass with microscopic star map

### Egg

- Confirmed as a real element (not just a silly idea)

## New Enemies

### Jack O'Rabbit
- **Appearance:** Giant fuzzy rabbit with a skull for a head. Uses "they" pronouns.
- **No shell** — but the skull head acts as a shield (defensive like a shell)
- **Clone Mechanic:**
  - Creates clones when attacked
  - Clones start tiny, grow to adult in ~2 seconds
  - Growth powered by Earth's magnetism — throw into space to stop growth
- **Weakness:** Speed Atmosphere (launches into space); during daytime "losing the only moon" knocks it out
- **Drops:** Rabbit meat, dragon skill, rabbit skull (summons 7 skeletons serving the dragon's master)
- **Combat notes:** Ramming = 1% damage; fire = more clones; Universe element would one-hit it
